# Operations and monitoring

Use redacted structured logs, correlated traces and relevant metrics to answer
real operational questions. Define signals from product requirements: failures,
latency, queue/backfill behavior and dependency health where relevant. Do not
invent SLOs or deploy observability infrastructure without need.

During active work/resume query fresh graph context, inspect blockers, changed
artifact hashes, stale evidence and actual external status as available. Reopen
affected work with dependency links and attach diagnostic proof. An earlier phase
may inspect monitoring signals without bypassing its own completion gates.

A monitoring record is a point-in-time observation. Persistent schedules,
notifications and background services require actual configured capabilities and
session authorization. Never promise always-on monitoring from installing skills.
